The active typography pairs **Product Sans** for headings and the wordmark with **DM Sans** for body copy, descriptions, links and controls. **DM Mono** remains for technical measurements, dates and data labels. The footer uses Product Sans for its name and inquiry heading, and DM Sans for supporting text.

`Product Sans Regular.ttf` (400) and `Product Sans Bold.ttf` (700) were supplied locally by the portfolio owner. These original binaries are served unmodified; their spaces are URL-encoded as `%20`. The bold face is the only preloaded font and is included in generated critical CSS. The regular face loads through the full stylesheet. Both declarations use `font-display: swap` in `assets/css/site.css`. The supplied italic files are retained but are not requested by the site.

DM Sans (400–700) and DM Mono (400 and 500) are locally served Latin WOFF2 subsets downloaded from Google Fonts on 2026-11-09. Their unmodified binaries and SIL Open Font Licenses are preserved here. `scripts/fetch-portfolio-fonts.mjs` records their pinned distribution and license URLs. They load through `assets/css/secondary-fonts.css` after full styling on desktop and direct section links, or after the first phone scroll, with `font-display: optional`. The no-JavaScript fallback loads both stylesheets normally. There is no Google Fonts stylesheet request.

The earlier Space Grotesk and Google Sans files and their licenses are retained as unused assets. Space Grotesk's original distribution is recorded by `scripts/fetch-portfolio-fonts.mjs`. The Google Sans Latin WOFF2 was downloaded on 2026-11-24 from its [pinned Google Fonts binary](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/googlesans/v76/4UasrENHsxJlGDuGo1OIlJfC6l_24rlCK1Yo_Iqcsih3SAyH6cAwhX9RPjIUvQ.woff2). Its SHA-256 is `df4ccb79a78e07ce44367a5ebcc7487bc58dd33b88a0c2a7e7db2735f4d6803e`; the upstream SIL Open Font License is preserved as `Google-Sans-OFL.txt`.

The welcome's Namaste is local SVG artwork and needs no downloadable font. Unused Noto language-family requests were removed from the homepage.
