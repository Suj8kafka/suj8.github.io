About Sujeet Khadka

I'm Sujeet Khadka, a designer based in Nepal, currently focused on UI/UX and visual design. My work began with a final-year school project and grew through personal experiments in mobile games, small web products, and interface design.

I learn by making: designing in Figma, building selected ideas with tools like Godot, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and using AI-assisted tools to explore and iterate. I am not presenting myself as a full-stack developer; I am a designer who enjoys understanding how an idea becomes a usable product.

Current Focus:

UI/UX design, visual systems, interaction design, Figma prototyping, responsive interfaces, and thoughtful use of AI during exploration and development.

Selected Work:

  • Carrom Karrom – A lightweight mobile game shaped by design, performance, and the realities of older devices.
  • Nakka – A digital interpretation of a traditional Nepali game, designed around nostalgia, accessibility, and offline play.
  • 2048 Dark Mode – A UX-focused redesign exploring dark mode, feedback, undo history, and more comfortable play.
  • Online Bus Reservation System – A final-year school project that introduced me to designing and building a larger web application.

I am still growing, but the direction is clear: make interfaces easier to understand, more enjoyable to use, and grounded in the people and context they are made for.
