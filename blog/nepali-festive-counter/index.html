Introduction

Nepali Festive Count is a simple and clean web platform designed to celebrate and keep track of major Nepali festivals. The idea behind the site is to make it easier for everyone to know how far they are from the next big celebration — whether it’s Dashain, Tihar, Holi, or any other cultural event.

Why This Site?

In Nepal, festivals hold deep cultural and emotional value. However, with busy schedules and digital distractions, people often forget the exact dates or countdowns to these moments of joy.

  • Shows countdown for upcoming Nepali festivals
  • Light and minimal UI with Nepali cultural colors
  • Responsive and mobile-friendly
  • Focuses on simplicity and clarity

Key Highlights

  • Real-time countdown to major festivals
  • Easy-to-navigate festival calendar
  • Dark mode for night-time viewing
  • Minimal yet culturally inspired design

Development Notes

The site was built using modern web technologies including HTML, CSS (Tailwind) and a bit of JavaScript for dynamic countdowns. Inspired by the Nepali spirit and festivals, it aims to merge cultural heritage with simple digital design.

The idea started as a side project to help users quickly check how long until Dashain or Tihar without opening multiple calendar apps. The clean layout and auto-refreshing countdowns make it easy and accessible to everyone.

What’s Next?

  • More festivals and regional event data
  • Festival stories and traditions section
  • Integration with Nepali Calendar (Bikram Sambat)
  • Widgets and shareable countdown links
