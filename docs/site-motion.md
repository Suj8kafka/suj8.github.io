GSAP 3.15.1 and ScrollTrigger are served from `assets/vendor/gsap/`. `assets/js/motion.js` is the single entry point; it starts after `loader:done` and also handles a missing/blocked loader. The first-visit nine-second intro, returning handwritten Namaste, portrait color transitions, native anchor scrolling, and speed-responsive viewport blur retain their existing implementations.

Hero and section entrance fades are disabled. Previously, delayed GSAP `from` tweens could hide content after it had already painted, producing a visible flash after loader dismissal or intersection. Text, portraits and cards now remain visible throughout startup and scrolling. Skill card colours are present in CSS from the first paint, with no intersection observer or timed colour resets. GSAP's media-query context still reverts depth styles and triggers when reduced motion or the layout changes.

`parallax.js` uses cached ScrollTrigger ranges for bounded hero, landscape, project-image and decorative-illustration depth. It no longer measures every layer in a custom animation loop. Pointer movement reuses `quickTo` tweens on numeric state, with no pointer tilt on touch, compact or constrained devices. Reduced motion creates no parallax. Anchor navigation and hidden tabs pause owned depth triggers; arrival resumes them without resetting transforms.

Slower devices use shorter depth travel and no pointer animation. GSAP failure leaves static content readable. The JavaScript does not add scroll smoothing, pinning or transform wrappers around the document, so fixed navigation, live cursors and the viewport blur keep their existing behavior.

`scroll-trail.js` draws a broad accent-colour ribbon through the page, with long S-curves sweeping across the background. The stroke is 32–64px on desktop and 24px on mobile, with a low-opacity tint behind the content. The rounded leading edge advances as the reader scrolls, leaving the ribbon drawn from the beginning; scrolling back retracts it. It replaces the narrow gutter trail and separate dot. GSAP ScrollTrigger scrubs only the decorative SVG; it never hides, pins or transforms content. No additional GSAP plugin, scroll listener or perpetual animation loop is used.

The SVG is rendered at the current scroll position before it becomes visible. Geometry uses the main element's width and height and is measured on refresh, including viewport and content-size changes, rather than during each scroll frame. Sections sit above the ribbon; fixed dialogs retain their existing stacking order. Reduced motion creates no ribbon, hidden tabs pause its trigger, and teardown removes the SVG, trigger and observers.
