Decorative enhancements wait for those assets and welcome completion; motion waits for first scroll on compact screens. Failed optional enhancements must not block the others, and presence import failures show immediately even if availability is stalled. `loader:done` is a startup signal; motion also handles load, visibility and preference changes.

First paint uses generated critical CSS, inline preference/assistant styles and the responsive portrait. Decorative note positioning and landscape sizing are present before full styling. The portrait gets an 800ms head start before the full sheet downloads; stalled image decoding cannot block layout. Interaction and direct section links prioritize styling. Activation follows two paint opportunities, with a 150ms fallback for suspended frames. Secondary fonts wait for first phone scroll. Homepage controls load through the deferred `portfolio.bundle.js`; the welcome loader and optional enhancement scheduler keep their independent lifecycles. ## Welcome contract

Preserve these normal-entry scenarios when changing startup or performance. A special replay URL is not a substitute for testing ordinary visits.

| Visitor scenario | Required behavior | Regression coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First desktop or phone visit, empty storage | Full greeting sequence ending in handwritten Namaste | `loader.test.mjs`: first desktop and phone visits |
| Regular returning visit, normal URL/reload | Brief handwritten Namaste; do not silently bypass the overlay | `loader.test.mjs`: returning visits and ordinary returning visits |
| Returning visit with a section hash | Brief Namaste while preserving the destination | `loader.test.mjs`: returning visits preserve section hashes |
| Back/forward restoration | Fresh brief Namaste, then release scroll lock | `loader.test.mjs`: restoration scenarios |
| Storage unavailable | Brief Namaste fallback | `loader.test.mjs`: blocked storage scenario |
| Reduced motion, Reader mode or Save Data | Bypass animation and keep content usable | `loader.test.mjs`: preference bypass; `display-preferences.test.mjs` |
| Broken/stalled painting, ink or optional assets | Finite recovery, working Skip, released timers/listeners/scroll lock | `loader.test.mjs`: stalled painting, skip and slow assets |

These expectations change only with a corresponding user request; an optimization must preserve them. Timing, artwork and generation details follow below.

- First-time visitors see Hello, Hola, Bonjour, Ciao, Hallo, then handwritten Namaste (roughly six seconds on desktop or five on phones). Regular returning visitors see only brief handwritten Namaste, normally about 1.5 seconds after paint. Browser back/forward restoration also replays a fresh brief Namaste. No special query parameter is required.
- Unconstrained first visits use staggered glyph entrances, a clear reading hold, large outlined word echoes, sweeping rules, orbital linework and theme-aware washes. A delayed greeting timer advances one word at a time instead of skipping languages. Returning visits and constrained devices keep lighter effects; Namaste uses the clear ten-path pen-stroke SVG.
- The controller is embedded immediately after the SVG template and before page content. It does not wait for a loader download, page load, image decoding or fonts. Both clocks and effects start after two paint opportunities. When available, native stroke completion also gates dismissal, followed by at least a 300ms finished-ink hold and a 220ms exit. A separate 2.5-second brief deadline, or first-sequence deadline with two seconds of recovery room, releases stalled rendering.
- Section URLs preserve their hash while showing the welcome appropriate to the visitor. Hidden startup waits for visibility before spending its animation. Hiding an already active welcome dismisses it. Reduced motion, Reader mode and Save Data bypass it; explicit Full motion overrides the OS reduced-motion default. No JavaScript leaves it hidden.
- A nine-second head watchdog releases the page if the inline controller is missing or blocked; late controllers cannot restart a released intro. Skip, scrolling/navigation input and visibility changes release timers, frames and native effects. Explicit Skip restores focus to the hero heading. Progress uses a native animation instead of a continuous JS loop.
- Run `node scripts/sync-welcome.mjs` after source edits, then `--check` to verify the inline CSS, controller, ten-path SVG template and exact CSP hashes. The matching security Worker policy must accompany publication; no permissive inline-script exception is used.
- The alternate `assets/js/page-loader.js` and `assets/css/page-loader.css` are inactive.

## Depth, portrait and navigation contracts

Content stays visible during startup and scrolling. Delayed entrance `from` tweens previously hid painted content; preserve this decision. GSAP/ScrollTrigger 3.15.1 remain vendored and unmodified. Use cached ScrollTrigger ranges, bounded depth and controller cleanup; do not introduce a document-level smoothing wrapper or per-layer layout reads on every scroll frame.

Compact, coarse-pointer and constrained devices use lighter effects. Reduced motion creates no parallax/ribbon. Hidden tabs and native anchor trips pause owned depth triggers; resumption preserves transforms. Reader mode and changed display preferences must stop/revert decorative work. The ribbon scrubs a decorative SVG and measures geometry on refresh; it does not pin or hide content.

Ribbon cleanup must also release its observer, listeners and SVG when GSAP's media context has already detached the tween's ScrollTrigger during a breakpoint or preference change.

Portrait fallback: responsive 440/640/880px WebP, original 1145×1527 ratio, matching preload/srcset, meaningful alt. Only unconstrained fine-pointer desktop visits with normal motion fetch the SVG after content is visible. Preserve the space in `assets/images/sujeet-hero- vector2.svg` and `%20` URL. Prefix SVG IDs when inlining. Pointer color waves use original path colors; focus reveals color, Enter toggles, Escape dismisses. Failure keeps the raster fallback readable.

Anchors use one native `scrollIntoView` request, with keyboard-aware heading focus and no extra scripted scroll loop. Scroll-speed-responsive viewport blur changes opacity, then hides when idle; reduced motion jumps instantly without blur. Repeated navigation replaces a trip; wheel/touch/pointer/navigation keys interrupt it. Completion uses `scrollend`, debounce fallback and a cleanup timeout.

The five-link bottom bar appears up to 800px, or 1100px with coarse pointer. Keep safe-area padding, 44px-or-larger targets, visible labels and native anchors. `aria-current="location"` updates are frame-batched; footer content must clear the bar.

The footer uses a 32-second projected-face loop: vertical-axis turns follow 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1; horizontal-axis turns follow 1 → 5 → 3 → 6 → 1. Faces 5 and 6 are top and bottom, not extra faces in the vertical-axis sequence. Each turn holds for 2.5 seconds, then reveals the adjacent face over 1.5 seconds. Complementary horizontal/vertical scales keep the rectangle filled and fixed. Face darkening, direction-aware edge lighting and easing suggest depth within the inquiry-sized box. Faces show six abstract interaction studies: grid alignment, Bézier curve editing, shape morphing, a spring press, connected flow and a moving-point ripple. `assets/js/footer-graphics.js` renders the shared vector primitives from `assets/js/interaction-studies.js`. The canvas renderer paints only the active/adjacent faces at up to 30fps, using cached dimensions updated by ResizeObserver. It shares the face-turn pause gate. Generated SVG symbols preserve static artwork when JavaScript or canvas is unavailable; reduced motion retains the static grid study. Motion is gated by `createFooterMotion` in the homepage bundle. It runs only while the footer intersects the viewport and the tab is visible. Its pause/resume control retains the visitor's pause across visibility changes. Reduced motion, Reader mode, Save Data, low device memory/core counts and missing IntersectionObserver retain a static box; no JavaScript also leaves it static. Cleanup disconnects observation, removes listeners, cancels canvas frames, releases canvas buffers and restores the SVG fallback. Verify lifecycle and geometry changes with `footer-motion` and `footer-graphics` tests. Regenerate the SVG via `scripts/build-footer-graphics.mjs` after geometry edits.

The footer explorer uses a 20-pose standing atlas and four overhead walk frames. `createFooterCharacter` tours both name rows, artwork, email, inquiry, navigation, social links and credits. Initial contemplation holds for 12 seconds; inspection acts last roughly 20–33 seconds, combining repeated framing, pointing and measuring gestures with long thinking poses. Travel averages 55px/s with smooth acceleration/deceleration, 220ms walk frames and slower camera changes. Hover/focus requests coalesce to the latest landmark and wait for the current journey and full act to finish. `footer-character-route.js` distributes stops relative to footer size and includes a safe bottom row for navigation/credits. Routes protect native controls and artwork, preferring to avoid typography; only the overhead view may cross display lettering when necessary. Standing stops stay clear of content, and the travel camera scales down at the boundary. ResizeObserver and font completion refresh cached geometry only while the character runs; offscreen, paused, compact and constrained visits defer layout searches until resumption; animation frames read no layout and paint travel at up to 30fps. Standing holds use one timeout. Existing pause, tab/viewport, reading and device gates remain shared. Pausing preserves progress and remaining pose time; a focused greeting control stays put until blur. The overhead sheet loads only when enabled; failures retain standing reactions and no JavaScript retains the thinking crop. Cleanup releases all owned listeners, observers, timers, frames and visual state. Verify with `footer-character`, `footer-character-route` and `footer-motion` tests.
